At Level 3, instead of choosing a Subclass from your Class, you can take the Paragon Subclass.

### Level 3 Subclass Feature

###### Novice Paragon

You gain a Class Talent of your choice from your Class.

###### Jack of one Trade (Flavor Feature)

You gain 1 Trade Point.

### Level 7 Subclass Feature

###### Expert Paragon

You gian a Class Talent of your choice from your Class.

### Level 10 Subclass Feature

###### Master Paragon

You gain a Class Talent of your choice from your Class.
