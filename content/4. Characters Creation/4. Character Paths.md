You gain Character Path progression at Levels 2, 4, 6, and 8. Each time you gain Character Path progression, you can choose the Martial Path or the Spellcaster Path. You gain certain benefits based on the chosen Path.

### Martial Path

**Combat Training**: Weapons
**Maneuvers**: You learn 1 Maneuver.
**Stamina Points**: You gain 1 maximum Stamina Point.

##### Classes Lacking Stamina Regen

A Class that starts without a Stamina Regen gains the Spellcaster Stamina Regen listed below when they choose the Martial Path for the first time.

**Spellcaster Stamina Regen**
Once per round, you regain up to half your maximum SP when you use a Spell Enhancement.

### Spellcaster Path

Combat Training: Spell Focuses
Spells Known: You learn 1 Spell.
Mana Points: You gain 3 maximum Mana Points.

##### Classes Lacking a Spell List

A Class that starts without a Spell List gains a Spell List of their choice from any Class when they choose the Spellcaster Path for the first time.
