## Combo Casting

**Prerequisite:** \*\*Spellcasting Path

During a Combo Casting, the creature casting the MP Effect is referred to as the **Caster** while the other creatures assisting are called **Participants**.

**Trigger:** You can see an MP Effect you know being cast or a Spell Duel being initiated, provided the MP Effect’s **Caster** or at least 1 of its target are within 5 Spaces of you.

**Reaction:** \*\*You can spend an amount of AP equal to the base AP cost of the MP Effect you are trying to assist (or stop in the case of a Spell Duel). When you do, the MP Effect gains a **+2** bonus to its Check and Save DC for this turn. If you are participating in a **Spell Duel**, the **Challenger** you are helping gains this bonus to their Check to determine the winner of the Contest.

**Spending MP:** You can choose to spend 1 or more MP to add MP Enhancements to the **Caster’s** MP Effect. The total MP spent on the MP Effect can’t exceed the **Caster’s** Mana Spend Limit.

**Example:** Casters A & B each have a Mana Spend Limit of 3. Caster A spends **1 AP** and **3 MP** to cast the _Fire Bolt_ Spell. Caster B can spend **1 AP** and **3 MP** as a Reaction to add 3 MP worth of MP Enhancements to Caster A’s Spell. Caster A then gains a **+2** bonus to their Spell Attack and Save DC for that Spell.
