### Bane

**Source:** Divine

**School:** Nullification

**Tags:** Curse, Enfeeble

**Cost:** 1 AP + 1 MP

**Range:** 10 Spaces

**Duration:** 1 Minute

Make a Spell Check against a the Repeated Charisma Save. **Save Failure:** The target is Cursed for the duration. While Cursed in this way, the target subtracts a **d6** from its Checks for the duration. The Curse can be removed by any effect that ends a Basic Curse.

**Spell Enhancements**

**Range:** (1 AP, Repeatable) The range of the Spell increases by 5 Spaces. You can use this Enhancement a maximum of two times.

**Targets:** (1 AP + X MP) You increase the number of targets by X.

**Full Bane:** (2 MP) The target also subtract the same die from its Saves. The cost of this Enhancement increases to 4 MP if you use the **Targets** Enhancement.

**Greater Bane:** (1 MP, Repeatable) The size of the die increases by 1 step (d6→d8→d10→d12).
