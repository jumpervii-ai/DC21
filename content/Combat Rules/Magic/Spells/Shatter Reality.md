### Shatter Reality

**Source:** Arcane, Divine

**School:** Enchantment

**Spell Tags:** Chaos, Dazed, Madness, Psychic

**Cost:** 2 AP

**Range:** 5 Spaces

**Duration:** Instantaneous

You violently distort the perception of reality in a 2 Space Sphere within range. Make an Area Spell Attack against the AD of every creature in the area. **Hit:** The target takes 1 Psychic damage.

**Spell Passive: Untraceable**

Damage from this Spell leaves no visible trace on any affected creatures or the surrounding environment.

**Spell Enhancements**

**Range:** (1 AP, Repeatable) The range of the Spell increases by 5 Spaces. You can use this Enhancement a maximum of two times.

**Damage:** (1 MP, Repeatable) The damage increases by **1**.

**Area:** (1 MP, Repeatable) The diameter of the Sphere increases by 1 Space.

\*\*Dazed: \*\*\*\*(1 MP) Each target makes a Intelligence Save. **Failure:** The target is **Dazed** for 1 Round.

**Distorted Perception:** (2 MP) Each target makes a Repeated Charisma Save against your Save DC. **Save Failure:** The target rolls a **d4** to determine the **Distortion Effect** that alters its body or perception for 1 minute.

| Roll | Distortion Effect |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | It sees its body twist - it is **Immobilized**. |
| 2 | Its senses collapse inward - it is **Blinded**. |
| 3 | Its mind fractures - it is **Stunned** and can’t take Reactions. |
| 4 | It becomes confused - At the start of each of its turns, it must spend **1 AP** to move up to its Speed in a random direction. |
