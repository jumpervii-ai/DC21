### Vampiric Touch

**Source:** Arcane, Divine

**School:** Nullification

**Tags:** Blood, Doomed, Healing, Slowed, Umbral

Cost: 1 AP

Range: 1 Space

Duration: Instantaneous

Make a Melee Spell Attack against the PD of a creature in range. **Hit:** The target takes 1 Umbral damage and you can spend 1 Rest Point to regain **1 HP**.

**Spell Enhancements**

**Range:** (1 AP, Repeatable) The range of the Spell increases by 1 Space. You can use this Enhancement a maximum of two times.

**Leeching Grab:** (1 AP) The target makes a Might Save against your Save DC. **Save Failure:** The target is **Slowed** for 1 round.

**Doomed:** (1 AP, Repeatable) The target makes a Repeated Charisma Save against your Save DC. **Save Failure:** The target is **Doomed** for 1 minute.

**Life Drain:** (1 MP, Repeatable) The damage increases by 1 and you regain **1 HP** on a Hit (You regain this HP without spending Rest Points).
