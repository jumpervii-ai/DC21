### Invisibility

**Source:** Arcane, Divine

**School:** Transmutation

**Tags:** Illusion, Invisible

Cost: 1 AP + 1 MP

Range: 5 Spaces

Duration: 1 Minute (Sustained)

You magically cloak a target within range for the duration. Make a DC 15 Spell Check. **Failure:** The target is **Invisible**. **Success:** The target is **Invisible** and can immediately take the Hide Action for free. The Spell ends early if the target takes any Action or Reaction beside the Move, Dodge or Hide Actions.

DC Tip: Being invisible makes you Unseen, but if you want to be fully Hidden from a creature you need to take the Hide action to also become Unheard.

**Spell Enhancements**

**Range:** (1 AP, Repeatable) The range of the Spell increases by 5 Spaces. You can use this Enhancement a maximum of two times.

**Duration:** (1 MP, Repeatable) The duration increases by 1 step (1 min → 10 min → 1 hour → 8 hours → Long Rest).

**Mass Invisibility:** (1 AP and X MP) You can target X additional creatures within range.

**Greater Invisibility:** (3 MP) Choose 1 target. The Spell no longer ends early when the target takes any Action or Reaction.
