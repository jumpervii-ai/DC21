### Psychic Imbued

**Source:** Arcane, Divine

**School:** Transmutation

**Tags:** Dazed, Deafened, Psychic, Strike, Weapon

**Cost:** 1 AP + 1 MP

**Range:** Self

**Duration:** 1 Minute (Sustained)

For the duration, you deal **+1** Psychic damage on Martial Attacks. 

**Spell Enhancements**

**Damage:** (2 MP, Repeatable) Your Attacks affected by this Spell deal **+1** Psychic damage.

**Effortless:** (1 MP) You no longer need to Sustain the Spell.

**Dazing Strikes:** (1 MP) Once per round, 1 target of an Attack affected by this Spell makes an Intelligence Save against your Save DC. **Save Failure:** The target is **Dazed** for 1 round.

**Silencing Strikes:** (1 MP) Once per round, 1 target of an Attack affected by this Spell makes a Charisma Save against your Save DC. **Failure:** The target is **Deafened** and can’t speak for 1 round.
