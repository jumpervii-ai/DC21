### Wind Tunnel

**Source:** Primal

**School:** Elemental

**Tags:** Air, Motion, Slowed

Cost: 2 AP + 1 MP

Range: 5 Spaces

Duration: 1 Minute (Sustained)

You create a 4 Space Line of forceful wind within range for the duration\*\*, d\*\*esignating one end as the Point of Origin.

- **Creatures:** Creatures in the area are **Slowed** when moving toward the Point of Origin, and move 2 Spaces for every 1 Space of movement spent when moving away from the Point of Origin.
- **Objects:** Objects weighing less than 200 lbs (100 kg) that aren’t secured or carried are pushed away from the Point of Origin to the other end of the Line.

**Gale-force:** When you cast or Sustain this Spell, make a Spell Check against the Might Save of each target in the area. **Save Failure:** The target is pushed up to 2 Spaces away from the Point of Origin. **Success (each 5):** They are pushed up to +1 Space.

**Change Direction:** When you Sustain this Spell or by spending **1 AP**, you can change the Point of Origin, reversing the flow of wind.

**Spell Enhancements**

**Range:** (1 MP) The range of the Spell increases by 5 Spaces. You can use this Enhancement a maximum of two times.

**Length:** (1 MP, Repeatable) The length of the Line increases by 6 Spaces.

**Width:** (1 MP, Repeatable) The width of the Line increases by 1 Space. The cost of this Enhancement increases by 1 MP for each time the Length Enhancement has been used.

**Distance:** (1 MP, Repeatable) A creature that fails its Save against the **Gale-force** effect is pushed up to 1 additional Space.

**Buffeting Winds:** (1 MP, Repeatable) Creatures in the area gain another stack of **Slowed** when moving toward the Point of Origin, and move 1 additional Space for every 1 Space of movement spent when moving away from the Point of Origin.

**Projectiles Tunnel:** (1 MP) Ranged Martial Attacks have ADV if made inside the area and away from the Point of Origin. Conversely, the same Attacks have DisADV if they are made inside the area and toward the Point of Origin.
