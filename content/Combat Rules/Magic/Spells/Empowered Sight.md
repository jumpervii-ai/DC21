### Empowered Sight

**Source:** Arcane, Primal, Divine

**School:** Divination

**Tags:** Embolden, Sense

Cost: 1 AP + 1 MP

Range: 5 Spaces

Duration: 1 Hour

You empower the eyesight of a creature within range for the duration. The target gains Darkvision 10 Spaces.

**Spell Enhancements**

**Range:** (1 AP, Repeatable) The range of the Spell increases by 5 Spaces. You can use this Enhancement a maximum of two times.

**Duration:** (1 MP, Repeatable) The duration increases by 1 step. (1 hour → 8 hours → until Long Rest)

**See Invisibility:** (1 MP) The target can see Invisible creatures within 10 Spaces.

**Piercing Sight:** (2 MP) The target can see through up to 6 inches (15 cm) of wood or earth (rock, dirt, mud, or sand), or 1 inch (25 mm) of metal within 10 Spaces.

**True Sight:** (4 MP) The target also gain True Sight 10 Spaces.
