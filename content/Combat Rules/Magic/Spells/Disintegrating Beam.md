### Disintegrating Beam

**Source:** Arcane

**School:** Nullification

**Spell Tags:** Death, Enfeeble, True, Weakened

**Cost:** 2 AP + 2 MP

**Range:** Self

**Duration:** Instantaneous

You create a 4 Space Line of pure destructive energy. Make an Area Spell Attack against the AD of every target within the area. **Hit:** The target takes 1 True Damage. The additional damage from Critical Hits and Heavy Hits or higher is doubled.

**Spell Passive: Annihilating**

Creatures and objects killed or destroyed by this Spell are reduced to a pile of ash.

**Spell Enhancements**

**Damage:** (1 MP, Repeatable) The damage increases by **1**.

**Area:** (1 MP, Repeatable) The length of the Line increases by 6 Spaces.

**Destructive:** (1 MP) The Spell deals double damage to objects and magical structures.

**Weaken:** (2 MP) Each target makes a Physical save. **Save Failure:** They are **Weakened** for 1 Round.

**Disintegrating:** (4 MP, Repeatable) Each Target makes a Repeated Physical save. **Save Failure:** They gain Vulnerable (1) to all damage for 1 Round.

### Disintegrate

**Source:** Arcane

**School:** Nullification

**Spell Tags:** Death, Enfeeble, True, Weakened

**Cost:** 1 AP + 2 MP

**Range:** 10 Spaces

**Duration:** Instantaneous

You conjure a bolt of pure destructive energy. Make a Ranged Spell Attack against the PD of a target within range. **Hit:** The target takes **3** True Damage. The additional damage from Critical Hits and Heavy Hits or higher is doubled.

**Spell Passive: Annihilating**

Creatures and objects killed or destroyed by this Spell are reduced to a pile of ash.

**Spell Enhancements**

**Range:** (1 AP, Repeatable) The range of the Spell increases by 5 Spaces. You can use this Enhancement a maximum of two times.

**Damage:** (1 AP, Repeatable) The damage increases by **1**.

**Destructive:** (1 MP) The Spell deals double damage to objects and magical structures.

**Weaken:** (1 MP) The target makes a Physical save. **Save Failure:** They are **Weakened** for 1 Round.

**Disintegrating:** (2 MP, Repeatable) The target makes a Physical save. **Save Failure:** They gain Vulnerable (1) to all damage for 1 Round.
