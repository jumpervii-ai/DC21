### Heal

**Source:** Divine

**School:** Invocation

**Tags:** Healing

**Cost:** 1 AP + 1 MP

**Range:** 5 Spaces

**Duration:** Instantaneous

You heal a creature within range. Make a **DC 15** Spell Check. **Failure:** The target regains **2 HP**. **Success:** The target regains **3 HP**. **Success (Each 5):** The target regains **+1 HP**. **Critical Success:** The target regains **+2 HP**.

**Spell Enhancements**

**Range:** (1 AP, Repeatable) The range of the Spell increases by 5 Spaces. You can use this Enhancement a maximum of two times.

**Increased Healing:** (1 MP, Repeatable) 1 target of your choice regains 2 additional HP.

**Chain Heal:** (1 MP, Repeatable) Choose 1 additional target within 3 Spaces of the original target. The additional targets regain **2 HP**. If you use this Enhancement multiple times, you choose an additional target within 3 Spaces of a previously chosen target.
