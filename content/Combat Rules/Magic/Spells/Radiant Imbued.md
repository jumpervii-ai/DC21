### Radiant Imbued

**Source:** Divine

**School:** Transmutation

**Tags:** Light, Radiant, Strike, Weapon

**Cost:** 1 AP + 1 MP

**Range:** Self

**Duration:** 1 Minute (Sustained)

For the duration, you deal **+1** Radiant damage on Martial Attacks. 

**Spell Enhancements**

**Damage:** (2 MP, Repeatable) Your Attacks affected by this Spell deal **+1** Radiant damage.

**Effortless:** (1 MP) You no longer need to Sustain the Spell.

**Illuminating Strikes:** (1 MP) Once per round, 1 target of an Attack affected by this Spell makes a Physical Save against your Save DC. **Save Failure:** The target sheds Dim Light in a 1 Space Aura and can’t benefit from 1/2 Cover or being **Invisible**.

**Guiding Strikes: ****(2 MP) Once per round, 1 target of an Attack affected by this Spell makes a Physical Save against your Save DC. **Save Failure:** Attacks made against the target add a d4 to the Check for 1 round.
