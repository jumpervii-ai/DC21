### Darkness

**Source:** Arcane, Divine, Primal

**School:** Nullification

**Spell Tags:** Shadow, Slowed

**Cost:** 2 AP

**Range:** 5 Spaces

**Duration:** 10 Minutes (Sustained)

You create a 2 Space Sphere of Darkness within range.

**Relocate:** When you Sustain this Spell or by spending **1 AP**, you can move the **Sphere up to 5 Spaces to another Space within range.

**Attach:** When you cast the Spell, you can attach it to an object or creature within range. If you do, the target sheds Darkness in a 1 Space Aura and you can no longer use **Relocate**. If the target is not willing, make a Spell Check against the target’s Agility Save. **Check Success:** The target becomes the source of the Darkness.

**Spell Enhancements**

**Range:** (1 AP, Repeatable) The range of the Spell increases by 5 Spaces. You can use this Enhancement a maximum of two times.

**Area:** (1 MP, Repeatable) The diameter of the Sphere increases by 1 Space.

**Duration:** (1 MP, Repeatable) The duration increases by 1 step (10 min → 1 hour → Long Rest).

**Light Eater:** (1 AP) Non-magical light sources within the area are extinguished.

**Grasping Gloom:** (1 MP) When a creature enters the area for the first time on its turn or starts its turn there, it makes an Agility Save against your Save DC. **Save Failure:** The target is **Slowed** for 1 round.
