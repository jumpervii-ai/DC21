### Absorb Elements

**Source:** Primal

**School:** Elemental

**Tags:** Embolden, Ward

**Cost:** 1 AP + 1 MP

**Range:** Self

**Duration:** Instantaneous

You conjure a protective elemental ward in response to damage.

**Trigger:** When you take Elemental Damage.

**Reaction:** \*\*Make a DC 15 Spell Check. **Failure:** You gain Resistance (1) to the triggering damage type until the end of the turn, this also applies to the triggering damage. **Success:** Resistance (2). **Success (10):** Resistance (3).

**Spell Enhancements**

**Amplified Shield:** (1 AP, Repeatable) The Resistance increases by 1.

**Flow of Nature:** (1 MP, Repeatable) You deal **+2** damage of the triggering damage type on your next Attack made until the end of your next turn.

**Lingering Retaliation:** (X MP) For the next minute, when a creature Hits you with a Melee Attack, it takes X damage of the triggering damage type.
