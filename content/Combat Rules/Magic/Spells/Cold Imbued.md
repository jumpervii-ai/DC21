### Cold Imbued

**Source:** Arcane, Primal

**School:** Transmutation

**Tags:** Cold, Slowed, Strike, Weapon

**Cost:** 1 AP + 1 MP

**Range:** Self

**Duration:** 1 Minute (Sustained)

For the duration, you deal **+1** Cold damage on Martial Attacks.

**Spell Enhancements**

**Damage:** (2 MP, Repeatable) Your Attacks affected by this Spell deal **+1** Cold damage.

**Effortless:** (1 MP) You no longer need to Sustain the Spell.

**Slowing Strikes:** (1 MP) Once per round, 1 target of an Attack affected by this Spell makes a Might Save against your Save DC. **Save Failure:** The target is **Slowed** for 1 round.

**Chilling Strikes:** (1 MP) Once per round, 1 target of an Attack affected by this Spell makes a Might Save against your Save DC. Save Failure: The target can’t take Reactions for 1 round.
