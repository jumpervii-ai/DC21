### Telekinesis

**Source:** Arcane

**School:** Astromancy

**Tags:** Immobilized, Motion, Restrained

**Cost:** 1 AP + 1 MP

**Range:** 5 Spaces

**Duration:** 1 Minute (Sustained)

You manipulate a target within range with telekinetic force for the duration. You can target an object weighing up to 200 pounds (100kg) or a Medium size or smaller creature. If you end your turn outside the Spell’s range from the target, the target becomes freed from the Spell’s effects.

**Object:** If the target is an object being held or carried by a creature, make a Spell Check contested by the creature’s Might Save. **Check Success:** You can use the Telekinetic Control Actions on the object for the duration.

**Creature:** If you target a creature, make a Spell Check contested by their Repeated Might Save. **Check Success:** The creature is **Immobilized** and you can use the Telekinetic Control Actions on it for the duration.

**Telekinetic Control**

**Telekinetic Movement:** When you Sustain this Spell or by spending **1 AP**, you can move the target up to 5 Spaces to another Space within range. If the target is a creature, the Space must be unoccupied.

**Telekinetic Throw:** You can telekinetically throw the target using the **Throwing Rules**. When you throw a target in this way, you are considered to be the same size as the target, you use your Prime Modifier instead of your Might to determine the maximum throw distance, and it’s thrown from its Space instead of yours. If the target is a creature, you make a Spell Check instead of an Athletics Check against the target’s Might Save.

Example: You take the Size Spell Enhancement allowing you to target Large sized creatures (such as an Ogre). When you use Telekinetic Throw on them, you are considered to be the same size as the creature for the purposes of throwing (Large in the case of an Ogre).

**Spell Enhancements**

**Range:** (1 AP, Repeatable) The range of the Spell increases by 5 Spaces. You can use this Enhancement a maximum of two times.

**Weight:** (1 MP, Repeatable) The maximum weight of the object you can target is increased by 100 pounds (50 kg).

**Size:** (2 MP, Repeatable) The maximum size of a creature you can target increases by 1 step (Medium → Large → Huge → Gargantuan → Colossal → Titanic).

**Restrain:** (2 MP) Targets **Immobilized** by this Spell are also **Restrained**.
