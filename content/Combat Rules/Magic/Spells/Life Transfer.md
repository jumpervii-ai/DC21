### Life Transfer

**Source:** Arcane, Divine

**School:** Invocation

**Tags:** Blood, Cleansing, Healing

**Cost:** 1 AP

**Range:** 10 Spaces

**Duration:** Instantaneous

You take damage to heal a creature you can see within range. Make a **DC 15** Spell Check. **Failure:** You lose up to **1 HP** and the target regains the same amount of HP. **Success:** You lose up to **2 HP**. **Success (Each 5):** Up to 1 additional HP.

**Spell Enhancement**

**Range:** (1 AP, Repeatable) The range of the Spell increases by 5 Spaces. You can use this Enhancement a maximum of two times.

**Increased Transfer:** (1 AP, Repeatable) You lose up to 1 additional HP.

**Transfer Rest:** (1 AP + 1 Rest Point, Repeatable): The target regains **+1 HP**.

**Affliction:** (2 MP, Repeatable) Choose a Basic Disease, Basic Curse, or Basic Poison affecting the target, if it is willing the affliction ends on the target and you become affected by the affliction.
