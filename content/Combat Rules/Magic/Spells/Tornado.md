### Tornado

**Source:** Primal

**School:** Elemental

**Tags:** Air, Chaos, Blinded, Motion, Slowed

Cost: 2 AP + 1 MP

Range: 5 Spaces

Duration: 1 Minute (Sustained)

You create a 6 Space high, 2 Space diameter Cylinder of swirling winds within range for the duration. Creatures in the area are **Slowed 2**, **Blinded**, and Fully Concealed. When a creature enters the area for the first time on its turn or starts its turn there, it makes a Might Save against your Save DC. **Save Failure:** They are pushed 1 Space horizontally in a random direction. **Failure (each 5):** They are pushed 1 additional Space.

**Spell Cast:** When you cast the Spell, make a Spell Check against the Might Save of each target in the area. **Save Failure:** They are pushed 1 Space horizontally in a random direction. **Failure (each 5):** They are pushed 1 additional Space.

**Moving the Cylinder:** When you Sustain this Spell or by spending **1 AP**, you can move the \*\*Cylinder up to 5 spaces to another Space within range.

**Spell Enhancements**

**Range:** (1 AP, Repeatable) The range of the Spell increases by 5 Spaces. You can use this Enhancement a maximum of two times.

**Area:** (1 MP, Repeatable) The diameter of the Cylinder increases by 1 Space and the height increases by 3 Spaces.

**Increased Push :** (1 MP, Repeatable) The distance pushed increases by 1 Space.

**Vortex:** (1 MP) When you cast the Spell, creatures within the area, or within 1 Space of the area, make a Might Save against your Spell Check. **Save Failure:** They are pulled toward the Point of Origin.

**Storm Core:** (1 MP, Repeatable) A creature that fails its Save also takes 1 Lightning damage.

**Updraft:** (2 MP, Repeatable) A creature that fails its Save is also pulled upward 3 Spaces.
