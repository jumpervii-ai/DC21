### Solar Beam

**Source:** Divine, Primal

**School:** Invocation

**Spell Tags:** Blinded, Burning, Light, Radiant

**Cost:** 2 AP

**Range:** Self

**Duration:** 1 Minute (Sustained)

You store a radiant power within yourself for the duration. You gain 1 Charge plus another Charge each time you Sustain this Spell (up to a maximum of 4).

**Sunlight:** Once on each of your turns while in an area of Sunlight, you can spend **1 AP** to gain a Charge.

**Spell Attack:** You can spend **1 AP** to release all stored Charges and make an Area Spell Attack against the AD every target within a 4 Space Line. If you release 4 Charges, you make the Attack with ADV. **Hit:** The target takes 1 Radiant damage per Charge.

**Spell Passive: Light Beacon**

For the duration of the Spell, the Light Level of any Space you occupy increases by 1.

**Spell Enhancements**

**Damage:** (1 MP, Repeatable) The damage increases by **1**.

**Area:** (1 MP, Repeatable) The length of the Line increases by 6 Spaces.

**Burning Radiance:** (1 MP, Repeatable) Each target makes a Repeated Physical Save. **Save Failure:** The target begins **Burning** for 1 minute. **Burning** damage from this Enhancementdeals Radiant damage.

**Blinding: ****(4 MP) Each target makes a Physical Save. Save Failure: The target is **Blinded** for 1 Round.
