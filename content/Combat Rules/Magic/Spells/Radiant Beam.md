### Radiant Beam

**Source:** Divine, Primal

**School:** Invocation

**Spell Tags:** Blinded, Burning, Light, Radiant

**Cost:** 2 AP + 1 MP

**Range:** 5 Spaces

**Duration:** 1 Minute (Sustained)

You create a 6 Space Tall, 2 Space Wide Cylinder of celestial light (sun, moon or star light) fills an area within range for the duration. When a creature enters the area for the first time on its turn, or starts its turn there, it makes a Might Save against your Save DC. **Save Failure:** The target takes 1 Radiant damage.

**Spell Cast:** When you cast the Spell, make an Area Spell Attack against the AD of each target within the area. **Hit:** The target takes 1 Radiant damage.

**Moving the Cylinder:** When you Sustain this Spell or by spending **1 AP**, you can move the **Cylinder up to 5 spaces to another Space within range.

**Spell Passive: Light**

The area is filled with Bright Light for the duration.

**Spell Enhancements**

**Range:** (1 AP, Repeatable) The range of the Spell increases by 5 Spaces. You can use this Enhancement a maximum of two times.

**Damage:** (2 MP, Repeatable) The damage increases by **1**.

**Area:** (1 MP, Repeatable) The diameter of the Cylinder increases by 1 Space and the height by 3 Spaces.

**Burning Radiance:** (2 MP, Repeatable) A creature that fails its Save begins **Burning** for the duration. **Burning** damage from this Enhancement deals Radiant damage.

**Blinded: ****(4 MP) A creature that fails its Save becomes **Blinded** for 1 Round.
