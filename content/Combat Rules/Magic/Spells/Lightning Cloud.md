### Lightning Cloud

**Source:** Primal

**School:** Elemental

**Tags:** Deafened, Disoriented, Lightning, Sound, Stunned

**Cost:** 2 AP

**Range:** 5 Spaces

**Duration:** 1 Minute (Sustained)

You create a 6 Space high, 2 Space diameter Cylinder of crackling lightning within range for the duration. When a creature enters the area for the first time on its turn or starts its turn there, it makes an Agility Save against your Save DC. **Save Failure:** The target takes 1 Lightning damage.

**Spell Cast:** When you cast the Spell, make an Area Spell Attack against the AD of each target within the area. **Hit:** The target takes 1 Lightning damage.

**Moving the Cylinder:** When you Sustain this Spell or by spending **1 AP**, you can move the **Cylinder up to 5 spaces to another Space within range.

**Spell Passive: Magnetic**

Metal in the area becomes magnetized for the duration, causing metal objects to attract or repel each other and disrupting compass-based navigation.

**Spell Enhancement**

**Range:** (1 AP, Repeatable) The range of the Spell increases by 5 Spaces. You can use this Enhancement a maximum of two times.

**Area:** (1 MP, Repeatable) The diameter of the Cylinder increases by 1 Space and the height by 3 Spaces.

**Damage:** (2 MP, Repeatable) The damage increases by **1**.

**Thunder:** (2 MP) A creature that fails its Save becomes **Deafened** and **Disoriented** for 1 Round.

**Stunned: ****(3 MP) A creature that fails its Save becomes **Stunned** for 1 Round.
