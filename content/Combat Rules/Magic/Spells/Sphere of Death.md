### Sphere of Death

**Source:** Arcane, Divine

**School:** Nullification

**Spell Tags:** Death, Doomed, Exhaustion, Umbral

**Cost:** 2 AP

**Range:** 5 Spaces

**Duration:** Instantaneous

You produce a burst of shadow magic that envelops a 2 Space diameter Sphere within range. Make an Area Spell Attack against the AD of every target within the area. **Hit:** The target takes 1 Umbral damage.

**Spell Passive: Obscure**

The Light level in the target’s Space decreases by 1 until the end of the turn.

DC Tip: Magical effects such as Obscure don’t stack with themselves. You can only decrease the light level by 1 with the Obscure effect.

**Spell Enhancements**

**Range:** (1 AP, Repeatable) The range of the Spell increases by 5 Spaces. You can use this Enhancement a maximum of two times.

**Damage:** (1 MP, Repeatable) The damage increases by **1**.

**Area** (1 MP, Repeatable) The diameter of the Sphere increases by 1 Space.

**Doom:** (X MP) Each target makes a Repeated Charisma Save. **Failure:** The target is **Doomed X**.

**Exhaustion:** (3 MP) Each target makes a Might Save. **Save Failure:** The target gains **Exhaustion**.
