### Gust

**Source:** Primal

**School:** Elemental

**Tags:** Air, Blinded, Deafened, Motion

Cost: 1 AP

Range: 10 Spaces

Duration: Instantaneous

You seize the air and compel it to create one of the following effects at a point you can see within range:

**Push:** Choose another creature within Range. Make a Spell Check Contested by its Might Save, you are considered as Medium sized for the purposes of moving creatures with this Spell. **Success:** The target is pushed 1 Space horizontally in a direction of your choice. **Success (each 5):** The target is pushed 1 additional Space.

**Move Object:** Choose one object that is neither held nor carried and that weighs less than 200 pounds (100 kg). Make a DC 15 Spell Check. **Failure:** The object is pushed 1 Space horizontally in a direction of your choice. **Success:** The object is pushed up to 2 Spaces. **Success (each 5):** up to 1 additional Space. This distance is increased by 2 Spaces if the object weights less than 50 Pounds (25 kg).

**Spell Enhancements**

**Range:** (1 AP, Repeatable) The range of the Spell increases by 5 Spaces. You can use this Enhancement a maximum of two times.

**Distance:** (1 MP, Repeatable) The target is pushed an additional 2 Spaces.

**Grand Gust:** (1 MP, Repeatable) Your size category for using Push increases by 1 (up to a maximum of Colossal) and the maximum weight you can affect with Move Object increases by 200 pounds (100 kg).

**Harsh Wind:** (2 MP) On a Success, the target is also **Blinded** and **Deafened** for 1 round.
