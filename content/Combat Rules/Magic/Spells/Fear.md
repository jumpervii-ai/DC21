### Fear

**Source:** Arcane, Divine

**School:** Enchantment

**Tags:** Emotions, Frightened, Motion, Psychic, Terrified

**Cost:** 1 AP + 1 MP

Range: 10 Spaces

**Duration:** 1 Minute

You instill fear in a creature within range that can see or hear you. Make a Spell Check against the target’s Repeated Charisma Save. **Check Success:** The target is **Frightened** by you for the duration.

**Spell Enhancements**

**Range:** (1 AP, Repeatable) The range of the Spell increases by 5 Spaces. You can use this Enhancement a maximum of two times.

**Inner Demons:** (X MP) While **Frightened** by this Spell, the target takes X Psychic Damage whenever it fails a Check or Save (not including the initial Save against this Spell).

**Flee:** (1 MP) The target spends **1 AP** running away from you each time it fails a Save against this Spell.

**Terrify:** (3 MP) On a Success, the target is **Terrified** of you instead.
