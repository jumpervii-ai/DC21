### Regenerate

**Source:** Primal

**School:** Invocation

**Tags:** Cleansing, Healing

**Cost:** 1 AP + 1 MP

**Range:** 5 Spaces

**Duration:** 1 Round

You heal a creature within range. Make a **DC 15** Spell Check. **Failure:** The target regains **2 HP**. **Success:** The target regains **2 HP** immediately and **2 HP** at the start of your next turn. **Success (Each 5):** It regains **+1 HP** immediately.

**Spell Enhancements**

**Range:** (1 AP, Repeatable) The range of the Spell increases by 5 Spaces. You can use this Enhancement a maximum of two times.

**Increased Healing:** (1 MP, Repeatable) The target regains **+1 HP** immediately and **+1 HP** at the start of your next turn.

**Repeat Save:** (1 MP) The target can immediately make a Repeated Save against an effect it is subjected to. It gains this benefit again at the start of your next turn.
