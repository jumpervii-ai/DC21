### Corpse Explosion

**Source:** Arcane, Divine

**School:** Nullification

**Spell Tags:** Ailment, Death, Enfeeble Umbral

**Cost:** 1 AP + 1 MP

**Range:** 5 Spaces

**Duration:** Instantaneous

**Trigger:** A target within range you can see dies.

**Reaction:** The target’s corpse explodes, make an Area Spell Attack against the AD of each target within 1 Space of the corpse. **Hit:** The target takes 1 Umbral Damage.

**Spell Enhancements**

**Range:** (1 AP, Repeatable) The range of the Spell increases by 5 Spaces. You can use this Enhancement a maximum of two times.

**Damage:** (1 MP, Repeatable) The damage increases by 1.

**Disease Cloud:** (X MP) A cloud forms in a 3 Space diameter Sphere centered on the corpse for 1 minute. When a creature enters the area for the first time on its turn or starts its turn there, it makes a Repeated Might Save against your Save DC. **Save Failure:** The target is Diseased for 1 minute. Creatures Diseased by this Spell have their current and maximum HP reduced by X at the start of each of their turns. The creature’s maximum HP returns to normal after taking a rest. This Disease can be removed by any effect that ends a Basic Disease.

**Creeping Death:** (2 MP) Requires Disease Cloud. Creatures Diseased by this Spell can’t regain HP.

Beta Note: A creature with a Medicine Kit can attempt to remove a Basic Disease from a creature within 1 Space (including themselves) by spending **1 AP** and 1 charge from the Medicine Kit \*\*\*\*and make a Medicine Check against the effect’s Save DC, removing the Disease on a success.
