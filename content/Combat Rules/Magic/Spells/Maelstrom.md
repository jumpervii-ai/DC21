### Maelstrom

**Source:** Primal

**School:** Elemental

**Tags:** Motion, Slowed, Water

Cost: 2 AP + 1 MP

Range: 5 Spaces

Duration: 1 Minute (Sustained)

You create a 3 Space diameter Sphere of swirling water within range for the duration. Creatures in the area are subjected to the **Underwater Combat** rules, begin to Suffocate if they can’t hold their breath or breathe underwater, and are **Slowed** when they move away from the Point of Origin. When a creature enters the area for the first time on its turn or starts its turn there, it makes a Might Save against your Save DC. **Save Failure:** They are pulled 1 Space toward the Point of Origin.

DC Tip: A Sphere’s Point of Origin is it’s centermost Space (or centermost 4 Spaces if it’s an even number of Spaces wide).

**Spell Cast:** When you cast the Spell, make a Spell Check against the Might Save of each target in the area. **Save Failure:** They are pulled to 1 Space to the Point of Origin.

**Moving the Sphere:** When you Sustain this Spell or by spending **1 AP**, you can move the \*\*Sphere up to 5 Spaces to another Space within range.

**Spell Enhancements**

**Range:** (1 AP, Repeatable) The range of the Spell increases by 5 Spaces. You can use this Enhancement a maximum of two times.

**Area:** (1 MP, Repeatable) The diameter of the Sphere increases by 1 Space.

**Increased Pull:** (1 MP, Repeatable) The distance pulled increases by 1 Space.

**Drowning:** (3 MP) When a creature fails a Save, they lose their breathe and begin Suffocating if they can’t breath underwater.
