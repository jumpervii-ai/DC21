### Close Wounds

**Source:** Arcane, Divine, Primal

**School:** Invocation

**Tags:** Cleansing, Healing, Time

**Cost:** 1 AP

**Range:** 5 Spaces

**Duration:** Instantaneous

You accelerate the natural healing process of a target within range. Make a **DC 15** Spell Check. **Failure:** The target can spend up to 1 Rest Point, regaining HP equal to the Rest Points Spent. **Success:** The target can spend up to 2 Rest Points. **Success (each 5):** The target can spend 1 additional Rest Point.

**Spell Enhancements**

**Range:** (1 AP, Repeatable) The range of the Spell increases by 5 Spaces. You can use this Enhancement a maximum of two times.

**Increased Healing:** (1 AP, Repeatable) The target can spend 2 additional Rest Point.

**Repeat Save:** (1 AP) The target can make a Repeated Save against an effect they are subjected to.
