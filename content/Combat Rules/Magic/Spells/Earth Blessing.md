### Earth Blessing

**Source:** Primal

**School:** Transmutation

**Spell Tags:** Embolden, Earth, Sense

**Cost:** 1 AP + 1 MP

**Range:** 5 Spaces

**Duration:** 10 Minutes (Sustained)

You imbue a creature within range with earthen magic for the duration. Make a DC 15 Spell Check. **Failure:** The target ignores Difficult Terrain on the ground and it gains Tremorsense 2. **Success:** The target gains Tremorsense 3 instead. **Success (Each 5):** The range of the Tremorsense increases by 1.

**Spell Enhancements**

**Range:** (1 AP, Repeatable) The range of the Spell increases by 5 Spaces. You can use this Enhancement a maximum of two times.

**Increased Tremorsense:** (1 MP, Repeatable) The range of Tremorsense increases by 2.

**Walk of Earth:** (1 MP) The target can transform Spaces into Difficult Terrain when it enter them. Creatures can spend **1 AP** to remove this Difficult Terrain from a Space.

**Burrow Speed:** (2 MP) The target gains a Burrow Speed equal to half its Speed.
