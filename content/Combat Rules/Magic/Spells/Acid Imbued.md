### Acid Imbued

**Source:** Arcane, Primal

**School:** Transmutation

**Tags:** Corrosion, Enfeeble, Hindered, Strike, Weapon

**Cost:** 1 AP + 1 MP

**Range:** Self

**Duration:** 1 Minute (Sustained)

For the duration, you deal **+1** Corrosion damage on Martial Attacks.

**Spell Enhancements**

**Damage:** (2 MP, Repeatable) Your Attacks affected by this Spell deal **+1** Corrosion damage.

**Effortless:** (1 MP) You no longer need to Sustain the Spell.

**Dissolving Strikes:** (1 MP) Once per round, 1 target of an Attack affected by this Spell makes an Agility Save against your Save DC. **Save Failure:** The target no longer benefits from PDR or EDR for 1 Round.

**Acid Strikes:** (2 MP) Once per round, 1 target of an Attack affected by this Spell makes an Agility Save against your Save DC. **Save Failure:** The target is **Hindered** for 1 round.
