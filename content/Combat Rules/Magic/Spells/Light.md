### Light

**Source:** Arcane, Divine, Primal

**School:** Invocation

**Spell Tags:** Light, Blinded

**Cost:** 1 AP

**Range:** 5 Spaces

**Duration:** 10 Minutes (Sustained)

You cause Bright Light to shine in a 2 Space diameter Sphere within range and Dim Light a further 1 Spaces.

**Relocate:** When you Sustain this Spell or by spending **1 AP**, you can move the **Sphere up to 5 Spaces to another Space within range.

**Attach:** When you cast the Spell, you can attach it to an object or creature within range. If you do, the target sheds Bright Light in a 1 Space Aura (and Dim Light 1 Space beyond that) and you can no longer use **Relocate**. If the target is not willing, make a Spell Check against the target’s Agility Save. **Check Success:** The target becomes the source of the light.

**Blind:** You can spend 1 AP and 3 MP to end the spell and make a Spell Check against the Physical Save of each target in the area. **Check Success:** The target is **Blinded** for 1 Round.

**Spell Enhancements**

**Range:** (1 AP, Repeatable) The range of the Spell increases by 5 Spaces. You can use this Enhancement a maximum of two times.

**Area:** (1 MP, Repeatable) The diameter of the Sphere increases by 1 Space.

**Duration:** (1 MP, Repeatable) The duration increases by 1 step (10 min → 1 hour → 8 hour → Long Rest). You no longer need to Sustain the Spell and can end the spell for free at any time.

**Intensify:** (1 MP) Light created by this Spell is considered Sunlight.
