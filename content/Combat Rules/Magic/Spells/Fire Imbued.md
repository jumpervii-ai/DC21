### Fire Imbued

**Source:** Arcane, Primal

**School:** Transmutation

**Tags:** Burning, Fire, Strike, Weapon

**Cost:** 1 AP + 1 MP

**Range:** Self

**Duration:** 1 Minute (Sustained)

For the duration, you deal **+1** Fire damage on Martial Attacks.

**Spell Enhancements**

**Damage:** (2 MP, Repeatable) Your Attacks affected by this Spell deal **+1** Fire damage.

**Effortless:** (1 MP) You no longer need to Sustain the Spell.

**Flame Lash:** (1 MP) The range of Attacks affected by this Spell increase by 1 Space for Melee Attacks, or by 5 Spaces for Ranged Attacks.

**Burning Strikes:** (X MP) Once per round, 1 target of an Attack affected by this Spell makes a Repeated Physical Save against your Save DC. **Save Failure:** The target is **Burning X** for 1 minute.
