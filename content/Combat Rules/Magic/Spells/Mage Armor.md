### Mage Armor

**Source:** Arcane

**School:** Conjuration

**Tags:** Embolden, Ward

**Cost:** 1 AP + 1 MP

**Range:** 5 Spaces

**Duration:** 1 Hour

You create a magical protective coating around the target creature. The target gains **+2** AD for the duration.

**Spell Enhancements**

**Range:** (1 AP, Repeatable) The range of the Spell increases by 5 Spaces. You can use this Enhancement a maximum of two times.

**Duration:** (1 MP, Repeatable) The duration increases by 1 step. (1 hour → 8 hours → until Long Rest).

**Greater Armor:** (1 MP) The target gain an additional **+1** AD.

**Heavily Warded:** (1 MP) The target gains PDR.
