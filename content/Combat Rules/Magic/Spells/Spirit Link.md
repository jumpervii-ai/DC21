### Spirit Link

**Source:** Divine

**School:** Invocation

**Tags:** Blood, Curse, Spirit, Ward

**Cost: 1** AP + 1 MP

**Range:** 5 Spaces

**Duration:** 1 Minute

Make a Spell Check against the Repeated Charisma Save of 2 creatures within range that don’t share their HP. **Check Success:** If both target’s fail the Save, they are Linked for the Duration.

DC Tip: A creature can choose to fail a Save if it want.

**Linked:** While within 10 Spaces of each other, whenever one Linked creature regains or loses HP, the amount is shared with the other Linked creature.

Example: You target yourself and an enemy, they fail the Save and you choose to fail the Save making you Linked to each other. When the enemy drinks a healing potion to heal **2 HP**, you instead heal for 1 HP each, splitting the healing between you.

**Spell Enhancements**

**Range:** (1 AP, Repeatable) The range of the Spell increases by 5 Spaces. You can use this Enhancement a maximum of two times.

**Extended Link:** (1 MP, Repeatable) The maximum range of the Link increases by 5 Spaces.

**Lion’s Heart:** (1 MP) While within range of the link, each target gains Resistance to **Charmed**, **Taunted**, **Intimidated**, **Frightened**, and **Terrified**).

**Transference**: (3 MP) Instead of sharing HP regained or lost while Linked, choose 1 target to receive all the changes in HP.

**Profane:** (3 MP) Instead of sharing loses of HP while Linked, both creatures lose the full amount of HP.
