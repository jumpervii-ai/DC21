### Mind Blast

**Source:** Arcane, Divine

**School:** Enchantment

**Spell Tags:** Dazed, Psychic, Sense

**Cost:** 2 AP

**Range:** Self

**Duration:** Instantaneous

You produce a blast of psychic energy that projects in a 2 Space Cone. Make an Area Spell Attack against the AD of every target within the area. **Hit:** The target takes 1 Psychic damage.

**Spell Passive: Untraceable**

Damage from this Spell leaves no visible trace on any affected creatures or the surrounding environment.

**Spell Enhancements**

**Damage:** (1 MP, Repeatable) The damage increases by **1**.

**Area:** (1 MP, Repeatable) The length of the Cone increases by 1 Space.

\*\*Dazed: \*\*\*\*(1 MP) Each target makes a Intelligence Save. **Save Failure:** The target is **Dazed** for 1 round.

\*\*Psionic Trail: \*\*\*\*(1 MP) Each target makes a Intelligence Save. **Save Failure:** For the next 10 minutes, you know the target’s exact location while it’s within 10 Spaces of you.
