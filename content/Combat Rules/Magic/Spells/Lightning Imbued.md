### Lightning Imbued

**Source:** Arcane, Primal

**School:** Transmutation

**Tags:**  Lightning, Strike, Trap, Weapon

**Cost:** 1 AP + 1 MP

**Range:** Self

**Duration:** 1 Minute (Sustained)

For the duration, you deal **+1** Lightning damage on Martial Attacks. 

**Spell Enhancements**

**Damage:** (2 MP, Repeatable) Your Attacks affected by this Spell deal **+1** Lightning damage.

**Effortless:** (1 MP) You no longer need to Sustain the Spell.

**Chain Lightning:** (X MP) Once per Round, when you make an Attack affected by this Spell, you can choose 1 additional target within 3 Spaces of the original target using the same Martial Attack for the additional target. **Hit:** The Target takes X Lightning damage.

**Static Strike:** (X MP) Once per round, 1 target of an Attack affected by this Spell makes a Physical Save against your Save DC. **Save Failure:** For the next round, the first time the target willingly moves or takes a Reaction it takes X Lightning damage.
