### Death Ward

**Source:** Divine

**School:** Invocation

**Tags:** Cleansing, ****Death, Embolden, Ward

**Cost:** 1 AP + 1 MP

**Range:** 1 Space

**Duration:** 10 Minutes

You ward a creature within range against death. Make a **DC 15** Spell Check. **Failure:** The next time the target takes damage that would reduce it to **0 HP**, it gains 3 Temp HP before taking the damage and the Spell ends. **Success:** The target gains 4 Temp HP instead. **Success (Each 5):** **+1** Temp HP. If a creature is already on **0 HP** or lower when this Spell is cast on it, the Spell activates when it next take damage instead.

**Spell Enhancements**

**Range:** (1 AP, Repeatable) The range of the Spell increases by 5 Spaces. You can use this Enhancement a maximum of two times.

**Duration:** (1 MP, Repeatable, Sustained) The duration is increased by 1 step (10 min → 1 hour → 8 hours → Long Rest).

**Additional Temp HP:** (1 MP, Repeatable) The target gains an additional 3 Temp HP.

**Doomed Protection:** (1 MP, Repeatable) Up to 2 stacks of **Doomed** ends on the creature.
