**Source**: [[Arcane]]
**School**: [[Astromancy]]
**Tags**: [[Teleportation]], [[Motion]], [[Slowed]]
**Cost**: 1 AP + 1 MP
**Range**: Self
**Duration**: Instantaneous

You teleport up to 5 Spaces to an unoccupied Space that you can see. If you’re [[Conditions#Immobilized|Immobilized]] the Spell fails.

**Spell Enhancements**

**Distance**: (1 MP, Repeatable) The distance of the teleport increases by 3 Spaces.

**Unbound**: (1 MP) The Spell no longer fails if you are [[Conditions#Immobilized|Immobilized]].

**Passenger**: (1 AP + X MP) X willing creatures (that aren’t [[Conditions#Immobilized|Immobilized]]) within 2 Spaces of you are also teleported, appearing in an unoccupied space within 2 Spaces of where you appear after teleporting. If used with Unbound, you can teleport other [[Conditions#Immobilized|Immobilized]] creatures. The AP cost of this Enhancement can’t be paid for with MP.

**Temporal Shiver**: (X MP) Creatures within 1 Spaces of you before you teleport make an Intelligence Save against your Save DC.

- **Save Failure**: The target is [[Conditions#Slowed X|Slowed X]] for 1 Round.

**Temporal Repulse**: (X MP) After you teleport, creatures within 1 Space of you make a Might Save against your Save DC.

- **Save Failure**: The target is pushed X Spaces away from you.
